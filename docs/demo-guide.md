# Demo Guide (6-minute assessment video)

Prepare to record yourself on camera for at least 6 minutes. Use the actual running app and verified links.

## Timeline (suggested)
| Time | Section | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00 - 00:55 | Introduction & problem | Explain why a cold-call assistant is useful for the Meridian Growth Partners workflow. |
| 00:55 - 01:25 | Dashboard & architecture | Show the dashboard, metrics, and explain frontend/backend/Supabase/LLM flow. |
| 01:25 - 02:30 | Start a call | Create a new call with a prospect and company. Show it appears and goes to the live assistant. |
| 02:30 - 03:50 | Eight-stage walk-through | Go through Opening -> Purpose -> Permission -> Their business -> Their need (record exact words) -> Objection -> Next step -> Close, saving and advancing. |
| 03:50 - 04:40 | AI copilot | At Objection or Their need, generate a real suggestion, point out provider/model/demo label and the caution. |
| 04:40 - 05:10 | Save, history, details | End the call with an outcome, reload to show persistence, open Call History and the call details with stage-by-stage notes. |
| 05:10 - 06:00+ | Tech & conclusion | Cover stack, security (keys server-side), testing/deployment, wrap up. |

## Step-by-step
1. Open the dashboard. Point out counts are from real data and the "Start a new call" action.
2. Click "Start a new call", enter a prospect (e.g. Senthil) and company (e.g. Kalyx Enterprises), create it.
3. On Live Call, walk each stage using the reference text: read the purpose, look at "what to listen for", enter the prospect's words exactly, fill what happened, pick an outcome, save/continue.
4. At stage 5 (Their need), record: "We want to push the new product line into two more states this year."
5. Use the AI Copilot to get a suggestion; explicitly say whether it is labelled demo or a real provider response and that suggestions should be reviewed.
6. Reach Close, click "End call", choose a final outcome, close it.
7. Reload the page and show the record is read-only. Go to Call History, search for the company, open the details page and show the prospect's words in the stage-by-stage view.
8. Mention the eight stages are from the official structure and the app follows the reference screen's discipline.

## Notes for recording
- Show your face for the full required duration.
- Do not fabricate URLs or deployment status. Only show what's actually running.
- If the LLM returns an error (quota/network), show the honest error and retry rather than faking it.
- Do not publish raw transcript files in the video.
